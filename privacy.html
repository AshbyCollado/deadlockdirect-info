Data and access

Privacy notice

DeadlockDirect is a Windows video-production app operated for the DeadlockDirect and DeadlockDirect Pro channels. This notice describes the current local app and its YouTube connection.

Prepared 29 October 2026.

Information used

The app uses YouTube API Services. With the operator's authorization, it reads the connected channel's ID and name and the IDs, titles, descriptions, thumbnail information, visibility and processing status of videos it manages. It sends selected video files, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, tags and publication settings to YouTube to prepare and manage those uploads.

Local production also uses selected replay metadata, player and hero names, match statistics, Twitch clip information, source credits, artwork and media files. The desktop app records local paths, job progress, errors, output hashes, episode revisions and approval decisions to support recovery and prevent duplicate uploads.

Google authorization

Sign-in and consent take place on Google's pages. The desktop app requests the YouTube account-management permission needed to upload and update videos and their publication settings. Google describes this permission on its consent screen. The app does not request Gmail or Google Drive access and does not collect or store the operator's Google password.

Each channel's OAuth client configuration and access/refresh tokens are stored separately on the operator's Windows computer using Windows user-level data protection. They are not placed in the application's project repository or sent to the local language model.

Where information goes

Media and production records are stored on the operator's computer. The app sends authorized uploads and video-management requests to Google/YouTube. Its source adapters communicate with configured replay and clip providers to discover and acquire source material.

Optional Qwen assistance runs locally through Ollama. It can inspect selected gameplay images, thumbnail candidates and production facts to suggest titles and thumbnail designs. The app does not send those requests to a hosted AI service. Generated suggestions remain subject to the app's checks and the operator's review.

The app does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. These static information pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising or cookie-setting code. Google's sign-in and YouTube services have their own privacy practices; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Publication control

Automatic uploads are private drafts. Public release or scheduling requires the operator's approval for the exact episode revision and destination channel. Material edits invalidate that approval. A channel connection alone does not approve publication.

Retention and removal

The operator controls local storage. Originals, final media and local job, approval and publication records are retained to allow review and recovery. Archiving a draft hides it from the active review backlog; it does not delete its media, local records or YouTube upload. Uninstalling the app does not automatically erase retained production data.

To stop future Google access, revoke DeadlockDirect in Google Account connections. Revoking access does not remove local copies or already uploaded YouTube videos. YouTube content can be managed separately in YouTube Studio. Contact the operator below to request removal of retained local data or discuss its retention. Local records and backups must be handled separately from remote videos.
