How YouTube access works

The desktop app uses YouTube API Services to check the selected channel, upload private drafts, update video details and thumbnails, and publish or schedule an approved episode. Each channel is connected separately through Google's sign-in page.

The app runs on the operator's computer. This website does not provide a sign-in form or collect Google passwords. Read the privacy notice for data storage, local AI assistance and access controls.
